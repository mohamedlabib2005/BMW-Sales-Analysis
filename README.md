# BMW Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive BMW Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides insights into BMW sales performance, revenue, quantities, yearly growth, sales channels, and top-selling and expensive car models.

##  Objectives

- Analyze BMW sales performance over different years
- Track total revenue and quantity
- Analyze revenue growth
- Identify top-selling car models
- Identify the most expensive car models
- Analyze sales across different channels
- Provide interactive business insights using Power BI

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

## Dashboard Features

### Main Dashboard

- Total Revenue
- Revenue Previous Year
- Revenue Growth
- Total Quantity
- Quantity Growth
- Revenue by Year
- Revenue by Sales Channel
- Top Sales Cars
- Top Expensive Cars
- Country-level sales analysis

### All Cars

- Car model analysis
- Revenue by year
- Quantity by year
- Quantity growth
- Interactive car selection
- Detailed yearly performance
